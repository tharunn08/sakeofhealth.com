Refund Policy

We stand behind every product we sell. Here's everything you need to know about refunds and returns.

✅ Summary: We accept refunds for production defects, expired products, or wrong items delivered. Raise a request within 7 days of delivery. Processing takes 10–12 business days.

1. Eligible Refund Conditions

Sake of Health will process a full refund in the following situations:

  • Production defect: The product is damaged, broken, or has a manufacturing defect.
  • Expired product: You received a product that was already past its expiry date at the time of delivery.
  • Wrong product delivered: The product delivered does not match what you ordered.
  • Missing items: One or more items from your order are missing.
  • Significantly different from description: The product is materially different from what was described on our website.

2. Non-Eligible Conditions

Refunds will not be processed in the following situations:

  • Change of mind after the product has been opened.
  • Dissatisfaction with taste or flavour preferences (we recommend checking our product descriptions carefully).
  • Allergic reactions due to known personal allergens listed in the ingredients.
  • Products that have been partially consumed (more than 7 days of use).
  • Products purchased from third-party sellers or unauthorised resellers.

3. How to Raise a Refund Request

To initiate a refund, please follow these steps:

  • Email info@sakeofhealth.in within 7 days of receiving your order.
  • Include your order number and a brief description of the issue.
  • Attach clear photos of the product (including the batch number and expiry date on the packaging).
  • Our team will review your request and respond within 2 business days.

4. Refund Process & Timeline

Once your refund request is approved:

  • Refunds are processed to your original payment method.
  • Processing typically takes 10–12 business days from the date of approval.
  • For UPI and net banking, the refund may reflect within 5–7 business days after processing.
  • For credit/debit cards, it may take up to 10–12 business days depending on your bank.

5. Return Shipping

For approved defect-based refunds, we will arrange a pickup at no cost to you. You do not need to ship the product back at your own expense. Our logistics team will contact you to schedule a pickup.

In cases where a pickup is not possible, we may ask you to ship the product back. We will reimburse reasonable shipping costs (up to ₹100) upon receipt of the return.

6. Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled before they are dispatched. To cancel an order, email info@sakeofhealth.in immediately with your order number. Once an order has been picked up by our delivery partner, cancellations are no longer possible.

7. Contact Us

For refund-related queries, reach out to:

  • Email: info@sakeofhealth.in
  • Sales: sales@sakeofhealth.in

We aim to respond to all refund requests within 2 business days (Monday–Friday, 10am–6pm IST).
